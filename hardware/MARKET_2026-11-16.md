# Market benchmark: professional lighting-control hardware (research only)

Fetched 2026-11-16. Method: maker datasheet/product page first (WebFetch, or pdftotext of the maker PDF); WebSearch standard mode for discovery only.
"not stated" = the maker source read this turn does not give it. No guesses. Retailer prices are marked as such (no maker gives MSRP unless noted).
Market leaders added: none beyond the list (no time to verify others; candidates not checked: ETC/Pathway, MA Lighting NDP, Artistic Licence).
Not verified at all: any firmware licence (open/closed) unless stated; Art-Net version unless stated.

## A. Art-Net/sACN to DMX nodes

### ENTTEC ODE Mk3 (70407)
- DMX: 2 universes, bidirectional (in or out), XLR5; isolation not stated. RDM: yes (E1.20, RDM over Art-Net).
- Pixels: none. Protocols: Art-Net (version not stated), sACN, ESP; HTP/LTP merge of 2 sources. MIDI/OSC/LTC: not stated.
- I/O (GPIO/relay/0-10V): none stated. Network: 1 x EtherCon 10/100, PoE 802.3af, WiFi not stated.
- Power: PoE or 12-24 V DC. Size: 129 x 58 x 40 mm, 0.10 kg; mount not stated. Refresh: up to 44 FPS; latency not stated.
- Price: not stated (maker page asks to enquire). Firmware: closed/not stated (v1.8 download).
- Sources: https://www.enttec.com/product/dmx-distribution/ode-mk3/ ; datasheet https://cdn.enttec.com/pdf/assets/70407/70407_ODE_MK3_DATASHEET.pdf

### ENTTEC Storm 24 (70050)  (DMX node, NOT a pixel controller; retailer lists it discontinued)
- DMX: 24 outputs (RJ45 ports), out only per manual; isolation not stated; RDM not stated. Refresh per port configurable 88 us to 1 ms (break); sync within 100 us (retailer-quoted datasheet).
- Protocols: ESP, Art-Net, sACN, KiNet. Network: 1 Gigabit Ethernet, no PoE stated, no WiFi stated.
- Power: IEC mains, any AC voltage (manual). Size: 424 x 207 x 44 mm (no rack ears) / 240 mm length with ears as printed. 1U-class.
- Price/firmware: not stated.
- Sources: https://www.allfordj.ru/upload/iblock/820/yp0ajjm9w2auruwnsvv0xtak3wor4w4u/rukovodstvo_polzovatelya_enttec_storm_24.pdf (maker manual PN 50479, mirror); enttec.com page 404 (no maker datasheet found)

### DMXking eDMX4 PRO (discontinued; replaced by eDMX4 MAX)

### Luminex LumiNode 4

### Swisson XND-8 (R3/R5/R8 rack, 19")

## B. Pixel controllers

### Advatek PixLite T16X-S Mk3 (current 16-output long-range; "PixLite 16 Long Range" is an older discontinued model)
- Pixel outputs: 16 RJ45 (to receivers up to 300 m); 1020 RGB / 768 RGBW pixels per output (expanded mode off), 510/384 on (32 outputs). 6 universes/output (3 expanded), 96 universes total.
- DMX: 1 x RS485 aux port, in or out; isolation/RDM not stated. Protocols: Art-Net or sACN (96 universes; Art-Net version not stated), 80+ pixel protocols, SHOWTime microSD playback, up to 25 triggers.
- Network: 2 x 10/100/1000 Ethernet; PoE and WiFi not stated. Power: 100-240 V AC. Size: 169 x 282 x 42 mm, 1.3 kg, aluminium, panel/wall/rack. Refresh/latency: not stated in datasheet text read.
- Price: retailers only, currency not stated in result (Digital Ambiance range 1,249-1,369; MossLED 1,799), unchecked. Firmware: not stated (closed assumed unchecked).
- Source: https://www.ac-et.com/storage/product_images/advatek/t16x_s_mk3/pixlite-t16x-s-mk3-datasheet_20250218.pdf (reseller-hosted copy of maker datasheet, dated 2025-02-18)

### Falcon F16v5

- Network: 2-port Ethernet switch (speed not stated); no PoE; WiFi ESP-07 module. Power 5-13 V DC pixel/external. Size, refresh: not stated.
- Price: USD 280.00 (maker store page, fetched 2026-11-16). Firmware: GitHub repository linked by maker; licence not checked.
- Source: https://pixelcontroller.com/store/featured/88-f16v5.html (product page; no datasheet found)

### Falcon F48v5 Differential

## C. Show controllers with I/O, timecode, triggers

### Visual Productions CueCore3
- DMX: 4 bidirectional ports, 4 universes (2,048 ch), opto-isolated; up to 32 universes with RdmNode/SpiNode. RDM: full controller.
- Protocols: Art-Net, sACN, KiNet, MIDI in/out incl. MTC/MSC/MMC, SMPTE (LTC) input, OSC, TCP, UDP, HTTP.
- I/O: 4 GPI (contact or 0-10 V, optically isolated); no GPO. Network: 1 Ethernet, PoE (page says class III; B-Station2/IoCore2 sheets say class I; conflict for CueCore3 in other sources), WiFi not stated.
- Power: 9-24 V DC 500 mA (PSU incl.) or PoE. Mount: desktop/DIN/19" adapter; size not stated. Refresh/latency, price: not stated. Firmware: closed/not stated (vManager).
- Source: https://www.visualproductions.nl/products/cuecore3 (no datasheet fetched; maker page only)

### Visual Productions IoCore2
- GPI: 8 (contact closure or 0-10 V, configurable). GPO: 8 relays, 50 VAC / 30 VDC max 3 A (maker page; retailers quote 250 VAC/2 A, conflict). 1 x DMX512-A in/out opto-isolated; 1 x RS-232.
- Protocols: Art-Net, sACN in/out, TCP in, UDP, OSC in/out. Ethernet 1 port, PoE class I; WiFi not stated. Power 12-24 V DC 500 mA. Mount: desktop/DIN/19" adapter; size, price, firmware: not stated.
- Source: https://www.visualproductions.nl/products/iocore2

### Visual Productions TimeCore
- Timecode: LTC (SMPTE) in/out, MTC, Art-Net timecode, RTP-MIDI; converts 24/25/29.97/30 fps. No DMX (needs CueCore). 1 Ethernet, PoE class I; protocols Art-Net, sACN, TCP, UDP, OSC, MIDI notes/MMC/MSC. 2 user buttons; no GPIO (IoCore add-on). Desktop/DIN. Power, size, price, firmware: not stated.
- Source: https://www.visualproductions.nl/products/timecore

### Visual Productions B-Station2

## Best in class (targets for the monster board to beat)
- Physical DMX ports on one unit: 24 (ENTTEC Storm 24); isolated bidirectional XLR: 12 (Obsidian EN12), 8 (Swisson XND-8)
- DMX universes managed by one controller: 100 (Pharos LPC X, via licence/nodes); direct pixel universes: 128 (ENTTEC Pixelator Mini MK2)
- Pixel outputs per box: 48 (Falcon F48v5); 16 with Mk3 long-range (Advatek T16X-S)
- Max pixels per output: 1,360 RGB / 1,024 RGBW (ENTTEC Octo MK2); 1,024 (Falcon, 32-port mode); 1,020 RGB (Advatek)
- Stated refresh: 100 fps (ENTTEC Pixelator Mini MK2); DMX 44 fps (ODE Mk3), 10-40 fps (LumiNode 4)
- Isolation: optical + galvanic per port (Luminex LumiNode 4)
- GPI inputs: 8 (IoCore2; 0-10 V capable); contact closures 10 (EN12)
- Relay outputs: 8 (IoCore2, 50 VAC/30 VDC 3 A)
- 0-10 V inputs: 8 (IoCore2); 4 (CueCore3)
- Timecode: LTC in+out, MTC, Art-Net TC, RTP-MIDI, frame-rate conversion (VP TimeCore); LTC in (Pharos LPC X S3, CueCore3)
- Network: 2 x Gigabit (Advatek T16X-S Mk3, Luminex LumiNode 4, Pharos LPC X); PoE 802.3af on ODE, Pixelator Mini MK2, EN12, LumiNode 4, S-Play
- Smallest/lightest: 129 x 58 x 40 mm, 0.10 kg (ENTTEC ODE Mk3); lowest stated draw: 1.5 W (MADRIX LUNA, datasheet) and 3.2 W max (Pixelator Mini MK2, PoE)
- Lowest published price: USD 270 (Falcon F48v5), USD 280 (F16v5), maker store; no show controller or node maker publishes a price
- Firmware: only Falcon links a public source repository (licence unchecked); all others closed or not stated
